Hi, we're John and Ani.

Furvival started with a question during hurricane season: if we had to leave in ten minutes, would Kala be ready? She wasn't, and neither were we.

Ani works in veterinary medicine, so we build each product around what veterinary professionals actually ask for when something goes wrong. Everything is designed here on Oʻahu.
