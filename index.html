Our story

Started at home, with our own pets

Furvival began with a simple question during hurricane season: if we had to leave in ten minutes, would our dog and cats be ready? Ani works in veterinary medicine and has seen how much a little planning helps when the unexpected happens. We started making the things we wanted for our own pets, and we keep going from there.
